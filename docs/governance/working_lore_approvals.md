# Working-Lore Approval Review

## Drift Clades (`fact-0004`) — 2026-10-28

**Author decision:** Keep the baseline established in
`lore/POLITIES_AND_FACTIONS.md` and `lore/SPECIES_OVERVIEW.md` as working canon.
The faction profile has `canon_tier: working_canon` and describes the Clades as
a loose grouping of low-gravity, memory-fragile peoples with fluid identities,
decentralized societies, void-drift risk, and memory-drive miniaturization
expertise. Their profile no longer labels them a single political faction.

Inheritance memory, recitation, and clade-traditional thinning are depicted in
primary `story-0004` (*The Light Inheritance*). Their scope in the wider Drift
Clades profile still needs separate claim review. The profile's unsupported
claims about compact reliquary innovations
and specific cultural doctrine were removed; they can be proposed again with
evidence.

The dependency report for `fact-0004` now finds four draft stories and six
speculative character or location profiles as direct dependents. It finds no
transitive dependents. The report follows structured links; prose mentions
still require review. `story-0004` now declares its faction link so it appears
in future impact reports.

## Primary-Backed Lexicon Definitions — 2026-10-28

**Author decision:** Keep `term-0004` Civic Memory, `term-0009` Eddy, and
`term-0027` Null Stone canonical, with definitions limited to claims supported
by primary sources. Civic Memory and Eddy now cite
`bible/ARCHIVISTS_WAKE_STORY_BIBLE.md`; Null Stone cites primary-canon
`story-0022` (*Spend What You Can Afford*). The Eddy definition no longer
requires a vortical shape, and the Null Stone definition no longer asserts a
universal transfer to everything it touches.

The dependency report returns zero dependents for these terms because it does
not trace lexicon `canonical_source`, `related_terms`, or prose mentions. This
is a coverage limit, not evidence that changing a definition has no impact.

## Story-0005 Lexicon Definitions — 2026-10-28

**Author decision:** Keep `term-0022` Continuity Cleric and `term-0023`
Liturgy of the Hull provisional as draft lexicon entries until `story-0005`
(*The Liturgy of Rust*) is approved. Their detailed definitions retain the
draft story as `canonical_source` for provenance and future promotion review.

the daily hull, decks, and seals recitation, but it does not establish the
draft's acoustic and focused-memory method or the effect of a cleric's doubt.

an association, not approval of the draft definition. The dependency report
does not trace `related_terms` or prose mentions, so these links and mentions
need review when `story-0005` is considered for canon.
