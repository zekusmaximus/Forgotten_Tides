# Story-0004 Review: The Light Inheritance

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Scope:** Targeted canon-readiness review of the full draft manuscript, its declared
story dependency, linked profiles and term, and overlapping primary stories.  
**Recommendation:** Keep `story-0004` draft. Do not run promotion until the
author settles the choices below and the revised story receives a fresh review.
The promotion dry run passes link-map preflight, which checks structure rather
than whether the story's claims agree with primary canon.

## Decisions for the author

1. **What is a light-bequest?** At `stories/short_story/the_light_inheritance/manuscript.md:261`,
   Yenu defines every light-bequest as a never-met, two-register inheritance
   that is unsafe to burn and may only be heard or released. The draft
   `term-0021` definition repeats that scope (`data/lexicon/terms.yaml:242-252`).
   Primary `story-0008` calls an ordinary personal-memory pledge for surviving
   granddaughters a common light-bequest (`stories/short_story/the_reliquarists_doubt/manuscript.md:146-148`),
   and primary `story-0022` places a named light-bequest into a civic silo
   (`stories/short_story/spend_what_you_can_afford/manuscript.md:686-689`).
   Its emergency use in `story-0022` need not be ethically approved, but the
   broader primary usage does not establish Yenu's universal physical rules.
   **Recommended:** Keep the broader category and make Yenu's stone a rare
   missed-generation subtype. Limit the second register, instability, and
   handling rules to stones tuned that way; frame release as Yenu's choice.
   Replace the claim that such stones *always* fail to reach their hearer
   (`manuscript.md:473`) with what Yenu or Tess actually knows. Update
   `term-0021` and check the inherited assumptions in drafts `story-0015`
   and `story-0021`.
2. **Which new mechanics should become reusable canon?** The story asserts
   that every Drift Clade pod uses a recitation-threaded coherence-frame with
   a regular retuning cycle (`manuscript.md:151-155`); that a stone can hold
   an intended-hearer-shaped absence and become a neutral *closed inheritance*
   after listening (`:261`, `:469-525`); and that a personal voice can be
   converted into a civic reinforcement field serving six thousand people
   (`:333-357`). Governing memory physics supports collective memory
   stabilizing large structures, but not these particular implementations or
   numerical claims. Decide which are local to this pod or stone, which are
   Aurel Ket's unverified pitch, and which merit a separately reviewed canon
   note and mechanics/lore update. The frontmatter's “no new metaphysics”
   assertion (`:59`) cannot stand with the current prose.
3. **Is Aurel Ket leaking a Consortium secret?** He tells Yenu a particular
   inner-ring node is currently drifting (`manuscript.md:337`), while
   `lore/POLITIES_AND_FACTIONS.md:100-101` says the Consortium conceals its
   failing nodes. Either treat this as a deliberate disclosure with
   consequences or make his offer about general reinforcement without naming
   the concealed failure. His conversion rate and duration should remain
   character claims until supported elsewhere.
