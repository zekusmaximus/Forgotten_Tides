# Nessa Iral

Nessa Iral works inside the Heliodrome's route-continuity machinery during the station's
post-intervention instability. Her formal role is junior continuity cartographer; in practice, she
is one of the narrow bridges between Heliodrome Authority public systems and Archivist witness
protocols.

She reads maps as moral documents. A clean route display can calm a crowd, but on the Heliodrome a
clean route can also teach the station to ignore its wounds. Nessa's first known major act occurs
in `stories/short_story/the_lie_that_learned_the_hallway/manuscript.md`, when she forces a damaged
residential hallway back into live route memory after Mael Ivar's reassurance begins outweighing
local clerk reports.

The correction costs her a private memory: the childhood route by which her mother taught her to
find home without public signs. That memory now weights a local route record. It does not repair
the Heliodrome, but it prevents one damaged hallway from being smoothed out of operational truth.

## Continuity Notes

- Nessa should remain a local procedural pressure point rather than a broad command figure.
- Her corrections can preserve damaged facts and help route discipline, but they should always
  reveal or redistribute cost.
- Future stories may build on her ugly map and her strained relationship with Authority messaging,
  but should not restore the personal route memory she spent.
