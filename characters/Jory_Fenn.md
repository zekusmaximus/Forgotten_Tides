# Jory Fenn
Obsidian Synod agent; Tessarine Vault night porter (five months)
First documented in *Spend What You Can Afford* (story-0022)

Jory Fenn is what the Synod looks like when it is not in the Black Horizon: a competent
man carrying trays with both hands, filed under competence by everyone who sees him. He
does not raise his voice, does not run, and does not resist arrest. His work is placed, and
when it is placed there is nothing left in him that needs to be anywhere.

## Doctrine as He Holds It

The civic silos of the Helios Drift are, to Fenn, not memory but burden: three generations
of weight set on living people who did not choose it, so that the people before them can
go on being true. Ending that payment is mercy. When an acolyte beside him falls through a
deck that has forgotten to hold, he says only that the man is lighter now. When Ilka Marsk
tells him that two hundred thousand people did not ask him to end anything, he answers that
they did not ask to be held either, and that this is the whole argument. She tells him it
is not an argument but a count.

## Operational Method (as established in story-0022)

- Infiltration through the pledge channel: null stones bedded in felt in ordinary pledge
  trays, reading as quiet stones until a cradle is lowered over them
- Timing to the quarterly pass, when every senior Reliquarist is in the hall with rings down
  and the upper silo doors stand open for review
- Lamps (null cores in dull housings, as in story-0012) carried by two couriers signed in as
  pledge porters

## Post-Story Status

Alive. One of two Synod agents in custody. Held in a storeroom off the Tessarine middle
ring under a porter whose hands no longer answer, awaiting the Archivist Inquisition.
