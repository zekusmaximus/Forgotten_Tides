# Elior Ren

Elior Ren is a new Canticle Fleet pilot first documented in *The Smallest Burn*.
He serves on a training rotation in the Helios Drift and performs his first
correction from the pilot cradle during a routine Memory Corridor run. The event
is operationally minor and procedurally successful; its consequence is personal
and permanent.

## Cognitive Profile

### Strengths

- Disciplined enough to re-check stored reliquary options before spending a
  personal anchor
- Precise in post-burn self-reporting, including losses the ledger might treat
  as negligible
- Maintains attention through correction and release before checking his own
  wound
- Accepts that small anchor losses still require exact record-keeping

### Vulnerabilities

- New to live corridor cost; first burn shifts his understanding of routine duty
- Early route-learning remains functional but emotionally thinner after the lost
  smell
- May overcompensate by becoming too clinical about future burns

## Anchor Structure

Twelve training anchor-knots at the opening of story-0013. Eleven responsive
anchors after the correction.

Burned anchor: the smell of his first classroom after rain: wet stone, carbon
dust, and bitter peel oil on wood. The teacher's face, the first route song, and
the factual structure of the classroom memory remain accessible. The smell and
part of the memory's warmth are gone permanently.

## Narrative Function

Elior demonstrates the cost of ordinary Canticle labor. His first anchor burn
does not save a station, stop an eddy, or rescue a passenger. It corrects a
routine corridor. The story uses him to show that institutional categories such
as "mild" and "basic sensory detail" can be accurate without being sufficient
to describe the change in a person.

## Post-Story Status

Alive, coherent, and cleared for continued duty after rest-cycle and Solace
consult offer. Eleven responsive anchors. First burn documented as permanent.
