# Sera Venn

Sera Venn is a junior auditor trained to believe that a record is a structural
obligation, not an ornament. In *The Error That Remembered Itself*, she discovers
that the Consortium can remember an employee's fault more readily than a Core
failure, and she chooses to make herself the carrier of the inadmissible evidence.

Her sacrifice is institutional rather than devotional or pilot-bound. She does
not burn anchors, restore memories, or create a new mechanism for truth. She
uses the Core's existing dependence on maintenance ledgers and personnel records
to make a concealed anomaly costly to delete.

## Post-Story Status

Sera's audit access, professional oath status, benefits, and housing are under
review at the end of story-0010. The maintenance ledger still carries her first
instruction and treats her as the initial observer for the node-adjacent seam.
