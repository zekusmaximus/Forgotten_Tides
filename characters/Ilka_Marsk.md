# Ilka Marsk
Navigant Sect Reviewer, Archivist Orders
First documented in *Spend What You Can Afford* (story-0022)

Ilka Marsk reviews other people's irregularities for a living. The Navigant Sect sends
her to Reliquarist sites on the quarterly pass to read junior seals, pull anomalous
stones, and enforce the prohibitions the Orders have written around memory expenditure.
She distrusts the word *possible* in any record and reads the doctrine lintel every time
she passes under one.

## Cognitive Profile

### Strengths

- Attention-binding discipline trained on dead corridor models; she can hold a null stone's
  local effect down by refusing to let her mind leave it
- Reads a failing structure the way she reads a bad corridor model: whole, at once, when the
  last line goes in
- Counts under pressure and acts on the count
- Records her own breaches with the same exactness she applies to others

### Vulnerabilities

- Has never, before story-0022, been asked to spend what she judges
- Her office gives her the sentence that stops a zero-anchor; she chooses not to say it
- Procedural restraint can read as coldness to people watching her decide who is released

## Physical State After Story-0022

Her left hand is present, functional under direct visual attention, and otherwise not hers.
The loss is a transfer of forgetting from a null stone into the body that held it, per the
established Structural Softening term. It is permanent. She must look at the hand to know
it has closed.

## Relationships

- **Brother Hael** (char-0029): met the morning of the breach. She permitted his final
  expenditure and scored his name into the Second Sleeve housing so the room would hold
  something of him.
- **Inya Solven** (char-0010): the junior whose seal she came to judge. Ilka watched her
  withhold one pledged stone from expenditure and ordered her to log it rather than
  surrender it.
- **Jory Fenn** (char-0030): the Synod infiltrator. Their one exchange after the breach
  ends with her refusal to treat his doctrine as an argument.
- **Elior Ren** (char-0017): the Canticle pilot who takes custody of the null stones and
  requires her to write what he expects to spend.

## Narrative Function

Ilka is the Orders' prohibition made into a person and then made to choose. The Navigant
Sect exists to say the sentence that stops a pilot from spending himself; she holds the
sentence in her mouth and spends two hundred thousand people's weight instead. Her report is
the story's last act: accurate, self-indicting, and signed under a doctrine line that has
lost its restraining word.

## Post-Story Status

Alive. Left hand lost to transfer. Report filed under her own name. Standing with the
Navigant Sect unresolved. Review of pledged stone TES-N3-44217 deferred.
