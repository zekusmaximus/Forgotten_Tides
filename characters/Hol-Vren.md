# Hol-Vren

Hol-Vren keeps the Skather-Ait pod's miniaturization shop and has been stretching
the frame's service life beyond its proper retuning interval. Yenu works for him as
a runner. He knows the frame's defects by measurement and by the places its residents
have learned to avoid.

In the draft continuation *Receipt for a Voice*, he closes the unstable sleeping
cells, then installs the delayed filament and records the true cost of its delivery.
