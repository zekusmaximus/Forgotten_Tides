# Hol-Vren

Hol-Vren keeps the Skather-Ait pod's miniaturization shop, where Yenu works
as a runner in primary *The Light Inheritance*. He taught her at eleven not
to touch softening walls. She remains an apprentice rather than a trained
tuner or drive-hand.

He has stretched this pod's overdue coherence-frame service life for two
seasons with calculations and temporary braces. He measures the bowed
sleeping-cell corner against yesterday's wax mark and moves the two youngest
into the galley. His brace remains at the story's end: filament replacement
and retuning are still unpaid, even when recitation firms the galley soft
place a fraction.

## Unapproved continuation

Draft *Receipt for a Voice* (story-0021) proposes a later closure of the
sleeping cells, a repair filament delayed by H-37's suspended certification,
and Hol-Vren's eventual retuning with Yenu's help. Its maintenance slate
records the materials, labor, route, and donor-memory cost. These later
events and additional training remain unapproved.

## Editorial guidance

Keep recitation and structural work complementary, with temporary measures
distinct from a completed repair. This pod's frame does not establish a
universal Drift Clade design or provide free, permanent stabilization.
