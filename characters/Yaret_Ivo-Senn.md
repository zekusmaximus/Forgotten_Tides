# Yaret Ivo-Senn

Yaret Ivo-Senn is a Drift Clade engineer and memory-drive miniaturist trained in a family
hand that values useful asymmetry, compactness, and parts small enough to save memory
without pretending to be elegant. In *The Miniaturist's Name*, she recognizes that her
family's compact drive throat work has been absorbed into Mnemosyne Lattice Consortium
patents without attribution.

Her defining choice is to make the Ivo-Senn line heavy enough for the Mnemosyne Lattice Core
to recognize. The correction protects the work from being renamed as Consortium property,
but it also turns a fluid clade name into a durable institutional handle. Yaret carries that
recognition as both repair and wound.

## Post-Story Status

Yaret leaves the Core with the Ivo-Senn lineage entered into heavy records. The corrected
patent can now cite the family hand as prior art, and the Consortium can also locate the
lineage more easily through claims, settlement offers, liability paths, and future
procurement systems.
