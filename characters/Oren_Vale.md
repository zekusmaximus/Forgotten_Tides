# Oren Vale

Archivist Orders field researcher  
Assignment: Remnant Seas relic census

Oren Vale works the kind of preservation the Orders prefer not to make ceremonial:
counting unstable remnants in places where claiming them too loudly can damage what
little coherence remains. He is trained as a field researcher rather than a pilot or
senior Reliquarist, with enough reliquary handling to distinguish personal, civic, and
stellar memory traces under extreme forgetting pressure.

## Cognitive Profile

### Strengths

- Patient census discipline in low-coherence environments
- Strong resistance to institutional appetite when evidence is incomplete
- Able to distinguish accuracy from usability in official records
- Trained in voluntary memory rendering and field assay procedure

### Vulnerabilities

- Carries guilt around omission and incomplete reporting
- No longer has access to the childhood star lesson that shaped his mapping practice
- His professional instincts are tied to preservation, but story-0011 forces him to
  preserve by refusing possession

## Story-0011 Status

At the opening of *The Quiet Census*, Oren is a functioning Archivist field researcher
with a stable personal identity and no stated anchor-loss history. By the close, he has
voluntarily rendered away the memory cluster that held both the shard's location and
his first star lesson from childhood. The loss is permanent as a story-local cost.

He files a limited report that preserves the existence of the question but removes the
usable extraction location. He does not keep the shard, sell it, publish full
coordinates, or deliver it to the Archivist Orders or the Mnemosyne Lattice Consortium.

## Narrative Function

Oren embodies preservation through non-possession. His choice is not anti-Archivist so
much as a hard reading of Archivist doctrine: some memory can only endure if no
institution is allowed to turn it into property, fuel, proof, or leverage.
