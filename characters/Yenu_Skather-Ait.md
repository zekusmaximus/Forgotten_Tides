# Yenu Skather-Ait

Yenu first appears in *The Light Inheritance*. She refuses to sell the gift made for her,
even though the pod's frame needs work. After her grandfather dies, she carries his
accurate recitation with the rest of her clade.

In the draft continuation *Receipt for a Voice*, she pays a different cost to bring home
the frame filament. The words of her grandfather's correction remain available, but the
sound she personally remembers is rendered into a stone and spent on a corridor.
