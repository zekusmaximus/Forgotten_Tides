# Yenu Skather-Ait

Yenu first appears in primary *The Light Inheritance*. She is sixteen, an
apprentice and runner in Hol-Vren's miniaturization shop on the Skather-Ait
pod. She takes her grandmother's missed-generation Light-Bequest to Tess
Vlin for an assay while the pod's frame needs repair. She declines Aurel
Ket's offer but authorizes Tess to approach private buyers, returning home
with a sale still possible.

Yenu then chooses to hear the stone beside her grandfather. Her intended
hearing closes this particular carrier. After his natural death, she
releases the spent carrier and carries his corrected account to her mother.
The galley soft place firms only a fraction during her recitation; the
sleeping-cell brace remains, and the repair is still unpaid.

## Continuation: Receipt for a Voice

In primary *Receipt for a Voice*, H-37's suspended certification delays the
booked transport of the pod's already-purchased filament. Yenu goes to Tess
Vlin at Halix. After an assay, she consents to render her remembered sound
of her grandfather's correction, retaining the conversation's facts and
her grandmother's voice. She chooses return of the unused stone if the
route is canceled, then declines to hear it before dispatch.

Elior Ren's cutter consumes her stone for the additional release-end
correction alongside ordinary drive stocks. The filament reaches Halix;
Yenu brings the crate home on the tether cup. She feeds filament into the
frame while Hol-Vren retunes it and corrects her work, then signs the
maintenance slate recording material, labor, route, and donor stone. The
corner holds, with further filament checks required. Her mother's later
telling supplies a different voice and further testimony; the rendered
sound does not return.

## Editorial guidance

Preserve Yenu's agency under financial and family pressure. Her chosen
hearing in story-0004 is distinct from the personal-memory rendering depicted
in story-0021. Corrected recitation carries an account forward without
undoing death or replacing the pod's material maintenance.
