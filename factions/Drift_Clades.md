# Drift Clades

The Drift Clades are a loose grouping of peoples from low-gravity, memory-fragile worlds, not a single political body. Their light frames and fluid identities reflect places where memories are short-lived unless reinforced. Their societies are decentralized and adaptable, and their worlds risk literal drift toward void margins.

Other polities often regard the Clades as unreliable. Working lore also describes their engineers as leading specialists in memory-drive miniaturization. The Clades are politically marginalized and struggle to maintain technological continuity as their cultures change.
