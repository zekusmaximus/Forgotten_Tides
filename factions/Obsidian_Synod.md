# Obsidian Synod

The Synod does not think of itself as a criminal organization. Its members believe they are performing an act of cosmic compassion — accelerating the universe toward the peace of universal forgetting. They are patient, methodical, and genuinely devoted.

The stations they have collapsed are not, in their theology, disasters. They are completions. The predatory eddies that form in the aftermath — spiraling voids that consume nearby memory structures — are, to the Synod, sacred phenomena.

The Archivist Orders regard them as the single greatest existential threat to the Memory Web. The Canticle Fleet hunts them when they can find them. The Black Horizon makes them almost impossible to root out entirely.

## Draft Continuity: Operations in the Helios Drift (story-0022)

*Spend What You Can Afford* (story-0022, draft) is the first on-page Synod operation inside
the Helios Drift proper and the first fictional use of null stones, the anti-memory cores
named in `lore/theology/OBLIVION_HERESIES.md`. The method: an agent (Jory Fenn, char-0030)
served five months as the Tessarine Vault's night porter and slotted null stones into the
quarterly review trays, so that the Vault's own tuning cradles amplified absence into the
audit hall while two couriers carried lamps to the civic silos. Two silos were lost to
oblivion. One acolyte died through a deck that forgot to hold; Fenn and the other acolyte
are in custody for the Inquisition. The Synod's conduct remained consistent with story-0012:
no spectacle, no resistance, a sincere account of release as mercy. What the Synod intends
next in the Drift is open.
