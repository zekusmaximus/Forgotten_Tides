# Skather-Ait Pod

The Skather-Ait pod is Yenu's small Drift Clade habitat moored in the Helios
Drift outer sleeve. Its physical hull relies on a local coherence-frame: a
low-resonant mesh of tuned filaments threaded with its residents' shared
recitations. This is Skather-Ait's design, not a universal Drift Clade frame.

A six-hour tether shuttle connects the pod with Halix Exchange. In primary
*The Light Inheritance*, this line shares a recited filament with two other
pods and a Remembrancer outstation. The shuttle has no Memory Drive; this
tether is not a corridor or an exception to corridor costs.

The overdue frame develops worsening soft places. Hol-Vren prolongs its
service with calculations and temporary braces. Filament replacement and
retuning remain unpaid at the end of the story: the galley soft place firms
only a fraction during recitation, and the sleeping-cell brace remains.
