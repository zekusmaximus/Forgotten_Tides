# Skather-Ait Pod

The pod-spine uncoupled from the lattice four generations before Yenu's childhood.
Its hull relies on a coherence-frame of tuned filaments, carried by the shared
recitations of its residents. A tether shuttle connects the pod with Halix Exchange.

The frame's long-delayed retuning is unresolved at the end of *The Light Inheritance*.
*Receipt for a Voice* proposes a later repair, recorded as draft continuity.
