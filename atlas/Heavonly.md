# Heavonly

Heavonly is a major political hub and gateway to three galaxies. The station is
dense with memory, authority, and traffic: Consortium officials, political offices,
wealthy traders, Archivist channels, cargo routes, and passenger corridors all
converge there.

The upper levels are polished and heavy. Surfaces have been remembered for
generations, public rooms carry institutional gravity, and official speech moves
quickly into record. The lower decks are less ceremonial: dock handlers, freight
auditors, recyclers, food vendors, maintenance crews, and gray-market brokers move
the physical material of civilization through systems that rarely credit them.

Heavonly's lower decks are important because they see the logistics of power before
the upper levels name them. Cargo from quarantined routes, anomalous manifests,
memory-market residue, and quiet administrative irregularities may all pass through
the workers who keep the station functioning.
